👑 Meet the Academy Faculty

Each professor has a classroom, a magical specialty, and a future role in interactive lessons. All faculty members are ageless, youthful-looking adults. All are women except Professor Onyx, the Academy's male professor. Their hair reflects the color of their namesake gemstones. These are original Academy characters.

👑 Headmaster Breezy

Keeper of the Silver Crescent

The Academy's founder and guide. Known for a silver crown, rainbow glasses, flowing auburn hair, royal purple and burgundy robes, and a love of amethyst magic. Oversees student journeys and the mysteries of the castle.

Future play: welcome ceremonies, quests, and graduation trials
