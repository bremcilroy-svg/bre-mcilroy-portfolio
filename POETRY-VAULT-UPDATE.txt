THE POETRY VAULT — CHAPTERS I & II
Bre McIlroy

THIS DOWNLOAD REPLACES THE EARLIER POETRY VAULT UPDATE
Two first-person rooms are now playable. The Heart Chamber unlocks four
poems and gives a silver mirror key. The Shattered Mirror uses that key,
adds four missing glass fragments, a restoration puzzle, a reflected
word lock, four more poems, and a clockwork gear.

Poems stay locked in the game until each chapter's puzzles are solved.
No "just read" button, reading bypass, or skip-to-poetry link is included.
Unlocked poems remain in the chapter's saved journal.

UPLOAD TO YOUR EXISTING GITHUB SITE
1. Download bre-lynn-poetry-vault-update.zip and extract/unzip it.
2. Open your existing bremcilroy-svg/bre-mcilroy-portfolio repository.
3. Go to the folder containing index.html and writing.html (the website
   root). Choose Add file > Upload files.
4. Upload ALL 19 extracted files at that level. Upload the files
   themselves, not the ZIP and not an enclosing folder. Replace matching
   existing files. Keep your other site files, photographs, and videos.
5. Commit to the branch your GitHub Pages site already uses. Your Pages
   settings do not need to change.
6. Wait for the Pages deployment, then refresh your website. Begin at
   Writing & Poetry > Enter The Heart Chamber.

If Chapter I is already complete in this browser, its opening screen
now offers Continue to Chapter II. Otherwise complete its puzzles and
inspect The Mirror Door. The ending links to The Shattered Mirror.
Continue in the SAME browser on the SAME site address; the key and
journals are stored there. A different browser, private window, cleared
browser data, or a different address has its own fresh progress.

CONTROLS
Computer: WASD to walk. Drag the room to look. E or the inspect button
opens the nearby object. Arrow keys move forward/back and turn.
Phone: hold the movement arrows, drag to look, or use the curved arrows
to turn. Explore objects provides the same puzzles without spatial
navigation, including keyboard and screen-reader access.
The mirror restoration puzzle has four dropdowns and rotation buttons.
Its visual borders also have text descriptions. Hints grow more explicit.
No timers or wrong-answer penalties apply.

SAVING AND RESETTING
Progress saves automatically in this browser. Each chapter has a separate
journal and reset. Resetting Chapter II keeps the Chapter I key. Resetting
Chapter I locks its poems and removes its key until you earn it again;
Chapter II's saved progress is kept, but its entrance requires that key.
The sound settings provide a confirmation before a reset.

SOUND AND GRAPHICS
Sound starts off. Optional narration uses installed English device voices;
a recognised female voice is preferred when available. No recorded narrator
track is included. Text remains available if the device has no voices.
The enhanced renderer uses WebGL2. A Canvas compatibility renderer uses
the same room, camera, and interactions on devices without WebGL2.
The test browser disables WebGL, so compatibility graphics were visually
tested; enhanced WebGL graphics were not visually verified there.
Mirror panels use decorative glass and etched shapes, not live reflections
of the camera or a player avatar.
