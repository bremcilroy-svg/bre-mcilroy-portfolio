THE POETRY VAULT — THE HEART CHAMBER
Bre McIlroy | Chapter I

WHAT THIS UPDATE ADDS
One playable first-person Victorian library room, four hidden letters,
a four-digit desk lock, the diary-symbol puzzle, a silver mirror key,
three-stage hints, a journal, browser progress saving, optional ambient
sound, device narration, and poetry unlocked through play. Keyboard, mouse,
and on-screen movement controls are included. Explore objects provides
an alternative to moving around the room.

The four poems are Falling in Love, Waiting for You, Don't Leave Me,
and Love Is Not a Feeling. Their manuscript wording is preserved.
The three remaining chambers are labelled in development.

PLAYING
Computer: WASD to walk. Drag the room to look. E inspects the nearby
object named by the prompt. Arrow keys walk forward/back and turn.
Phone: hold the on-screen arrows; drag to look. Curved arrows turn.
Explore objects lets you inspect everything without navigating in 3D.
Hints become more explicit each time. Nothing is timed.
Poems stay locked until you collect all four letters, solve the desk
lock, and complete the diary-symbol puzzle. There is no reading bypass.
After unlocking, revisit the poems from your saved journal.
Progress saves in this browser only. Settings > Reset progress starts
this chapter over after a confirmation. No account is required.

SOUND AND GRAPHICS
Sound is off by default. Enable ambient sound or narration in settings.
Narration uses installed English device voices. A recognised female
voice is preferred when available. Some browsers have no device voices;
all story text is also available on screen. No recorded narrator track
is included in this version.
The enhanced renderer uses WebGL2. A Canvas compatibility renderer uses
the same room, camera, movement, and interactions when WebGL is missing.
Compatibility graphics can be slower and simpler on older devices.
The completed puzzle path, incorrect-answer feedback, locked journal,
progress reload, and narrow layouts were tested in the browser.
The test browser disables WebGL, so its enhanced graphics were not
visually verified; the compatibility renderer was tested instead.

TEST LOCALLY
Use an HTTP preview/server. Opening the HTML directly as a file can
block JavaScript modules. GitHub Pages serves these files correctly.
Keep all included JS, CSS, JPG, and HTML files at the same directory
level. No build tools, external CDNs, or secret API keys are required.
Poems are public site assets; the puzzles are an experience, not DRM.
