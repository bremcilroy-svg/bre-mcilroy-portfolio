THE POETRY VAULT — CHAPTERS I, II & III
Bre McIlroy

THIS REPLACES THE EARLIER POETRY VAULT UPDATE
Three first-person chapters are now playable, with twelve original poems.
Each chapter keeps its poem pages locked until its puzzles are completed.
There is no direct reading or skip-to-poetry option.
The Door to Tomorrow, Chapter IV, remains in development.

CONTINUE WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
If Chapter I is complete in this browser, its opening screen offers
Continue to Chapter II. If Chapter II is complete, its opening screen
now offers Continue to Chapter III.
Inspect The Mirror Door at the end of Chapter I to earn the silver key.
Inspect The Clockwork Door at the end of Chapter II to carry the gear
into The Midnight Labyrinth. The chapter endings link to the next room.
Stay on the SAME browser and SAME site address so these items carry over.
A different browser, a private window, cleared browser data, or another
site address has its own fresh progress. Chapter handoffs require browser
storage; there is no account or cloud save.

TEXT AND CLUES
All twelve poem bodies preserve the words, punctuation, and line breaks
of the supplied manuscript. Short content notes appear outside selected
poems. The new puzzles use fictional object clues and ledger dates;
players do not have to interpret a poem's subject matter to solve them.
Unlocks remain in each chapter's saved journal.

CONTROLS
Computer: WASD to walk. Drag the room to look. E or the inspect button
opens the nearby object. Arrow keys move forward/back and turn.
Phone: hold the movement arrows, drag to look, or use the curved arrows
to turn. Explore objects gives access to the same objects and puzzles
without navigating in 3D, including keyboard and screen-reader access.
The clock has hour/minute dropdowns and a text readout as well as hands.
The ledger shows each page's date in text. Controls keep their focus and
scroll position when you change a value.
Hints become clearer each time. There are no timers or wrong-answer
penalties. No poem can be opened before its chapter's puzzles are complete.

THE LABYRINTH PATH
The passages alternate: right at the first opening, left at the second,
then right to reach the final gallery. Lanterns mark the turns. The
caretaker's note is on a table near the entrance. Pages sit on tables
along the route. The clock, ledger, and Sunrise Door are in the last gallery.
Explore objects is available throughout, with the same puzzle requirements.

SAVES AND RESETS
Every chapter saves separately in this browser. Resetting a chapter locks
its poems again and resets only that chapter's progress. Earlier chapter
items and journals remain saved. Resetting an earlier chapter may remove
an item required to enter the next one until you earn that item again;
the next chapter's saved progress is kept. Settings asks for confirmation
before a reset. Unlocked poems are never available in a fresh save.

SOUND AND GRAPHICS
Sound starts off. Ambient audio and narration are optional. Narration uses
English voices installed on the device; a recognised female voice is
preferred when available. This download includes no recorded narrator
track. All story instructions remain on screen if a voice is unavailable.
The enhanced renderer uses WebGL2. A Canvas compatibility renderer provides
the same room, camera, and interactions when WebGL2 is unavailable. It can
look simpler or run more slowly on older devices. The test browser disables
WebGL, so compatibility graphics were visually tested; enhanced WebGL
rendering was not visually verified there.
Mirror panels use decorative glass, not live player-camera reflections.
