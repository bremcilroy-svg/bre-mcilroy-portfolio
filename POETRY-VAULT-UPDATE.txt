THE POETRY VAULT — ALL FOUR CHAPTERS / CINEMATIC EDITION
Bre McIlroy

THIS REPLACES THE EARLIER POETRY VAULT UPDATE
Four first-person chapters are now playable, with sixteen original poems.
Each chapter keeps its poem pages locked until its puzzles are completed.
There is no direct reading or skip-to-poetry option.
The Door to Tomorrow, Chapter IV, completes the journey.
This edition adds graphics/movement improvements, animated interactions,
optional layered sound, and replay keepsakes. Existing chapter saves are
compatible; no reset is required after uploading.

2. Open your bremcilroy-svg/bre-mcilroy-portfolio repository.
3. Go to the folder containing index.html and writing.html (the website

5. Commit to the branch your GitHub Pages site already uses. You do not
   need to change your Pages settings.
6. Wait for the Pages deployment to finish, then refresh your website.
   Begin at Writing & Poetry > Enter The Heart Chamber.

CONTINUE WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
If Chapter I is complete in this browser, its opening screen offers
Continue to Chapter II. If Chapter II is complete, its opening screen
now offers Continue to Chapter III. If Chapter III is complete, choose
Continue to the final chapter on its opening screen.
Inspect The Mirror Door at the end of Chapter I to earn the silver key.
Inspect The Clockwork Door at the end of Chapter II to carry the gear
into The Midnight Labyrinth. Inspect the Sunrise Door at the end of
Chapter III to earn the sunrise emblem, then choose Enter The Door to
Tomorrow. The chapter endings link to the next room.
Stay on the SAME browser and SAME site address so these items carry over.
A different browser, a private window, cleared browser data, or another
site address has its own fresh progress. Chapter handoffs require browser
storage; there is no account or cloud save.

PLAYABLE CHAPTERS
I / The Heart Chamber
Find four sealed letters, solve the desk lock, and restore the diary.
Reward: four poems and a silver mirror key.
  Falling in Love
  Waiting for You
  Don't Leave Me
  Love Is Not a Feeling

II / The Shattered Mirror
Use the key, collect the missing fragments, restore the silver borders,
and solve the reflected word.
Reward: four poems and a clockwork gear.
  Not Meant to Be
  I Want to Be Loved
  Or So I Thought
  Darkness

III / The Midnight Labyrinth
Carry the gear into winding archive passages. Read the caretaker's note,
repair the clock, set its hands, collect four dated pages, and restore
chronological order in the ledger.
Reward: four poems and a sunrise emblem for the final chapter.
  Late Night Thoughts
  My Dear Friend, Ana Rex Sia
  Sold My Soul
  A Note from the Poor

IV / The Door to Tomorrow
Carry the sunrise emblem into a Victorian morning conservatory. Fit the
emblem, arrange and rotate four window panes, collect four released pages,
and bind them in the order described by their clues.
Reward: the final four poems, the last door, and the complete ending.
  Afraid of Change
  Moving On
  Never
  Self Love

After the final book opens, reading is optional. Inspect the final door to
finish the game. The whole journey panel links back to each chapter's saved
journal. The final chapter can be reset without resetting earlier journals
or the sunrise emblem earned in Chapter III.

TEXT AND CLUES
All sixteen poem bodies preserve the words, punctuation, and line breaks
of the supplied manuscript. Short content notes appear outside selected
poems. The new puzzles use fictional object clues and ledger dates;
players do not have to interpret a poem's subject matter to solve them.
Unlocks remain in each chapter's saved journal.

CONTROLS
Computer: WASD to walk. Drag the room to look. E or the inspect button
opens the nearby object. Arrow keys move forward/back and turn.
Phone: hold the movement arrows, drag to look, or use the curved arrows
to turn. Explore objects gives access to the same objects and puzzles
without navigating in 3D, including keyboard and screen-reader access.
The clock has hour/minute dropdowns and a text readout as well as hands.
The ledger shows each page's date in text. The final window has numbered
panes, dropdowns, rotation buttons, and text orientation labels. The final
book repeats the page clues beside its controls. Controls keep their focus and
scroll position when you change a value.
Hints become clearer each time. There are no timers or wrong-answer
penalties. No poem can be opened before its chapter's puzzles are complete.

THE LABYRINTH PATH
The passages alternate: right at the first opening, left at the second,
then right to reach the final gallery. Lanterns mark the turns. The
caretaker's note is on a table near the entrance. Pages sit on tables
along the route. The clock, ledger, and Sunrise Door are in the last gallery.
Explore objects is available throughout, with the same puzzle requirements.

SAVES AND RESETS
Every chapter saves separately in this browser. Resetting a chapter locks
its poems again and resets only that chapter's progress. Earlier chapter
items and journals remain saved. Resetting an earlier chapter may remove
an item required to enter the next one until you earn that item again;
the next chapter's saved progress is kept. Settings asks for confirmation
before a reset. Unlocked poems are never available in a fresh save.

THE FOUR CINEMATIC UPGRADES
1. Graphics and movement
The compatibility renderer draws flat faces as quads, overlaps texture
edges to avoid diagonal seams, caches geometry, and reduces its internal
resolution on large screens. World transforms remain live for animation.
Movement uses elapsed time, buffered quick taps, equal straight/diagonal
speeds, and short collision steps. Camera bobbing is removed. WASD, arrow
keys, drag-to-look, touch buttons, and Explore objects remain available.
The GPU renderer caps its pixel ratio at 1.4 to reduce phone rendering work.

2. Room interactions
Hinged doors open in all four rooms. Diaries and books open into visible
paper spreads. The restored mirror brightens. New poem pages transition
in gently, and puzzle/door completions have a soft light effect. Saved
completed objects restore immediately. The device's reduced-motion setting
skips movement effects and animations. Mirror glass is decorative; it is
not a webcam image or a real-time reflection.

3. Sound and narration
Sound starts off for a new player. Settings provides separate ambience,
effects, gentle music, and spoken-story switches, plus volume, narrator
selection, Test narrator, Replay story narration, Stop narration, and Mute
all. Each room has locally generated ambience; optional effects include
footsteps, doors, page rustles, collection chimes, and puzzle-completion
notes. No external audio service, paid subscription, or recorded narrator
track is included. Choices carry between chapters in this browser.
Narration uses installed English device voices, preferring a recognised
female voice when available. Late-arriving device voices update the settings
without reloading the page. If voices are unavailable, a message explains
this and voice controls stay disabled. After a poem is unlocked, its reader
includes optional Read this poem aloud and Stop reading controls.
Audio pauses when the page is hidden. Muting stops narration and sound.

4. Replay keepsakes
One optional physical bookmark is placed in each room and is also available
through Explore objects. These hold fictional library inscriptions, not
poem excerpts. Collecting them never unlocks a poem or changes a puzzle.
Open Keepsakes & discoveries from a journal, chapter ending, or Settings.
Completion stamps derive from existing chapter progress, so earlier saves
are recognised. Find all four bookmarks to earn Keeper of the Margins.
Complete all four chapters to earn The Whole Story and a downloadable SVG
completion keepsake. Its optional name is used only on the player's device.
This private journey overview has no public scores or leaderboard. Hints
do not reduce rewards. A reset removes that chapter's current completion
stamp and locks its poems; previously collected bookmarks remain saved.

GRAPHICS AND VOICE TEST LIMITS
Compatibility graphics were visually tested in the browser at desktop and
320/390 CSS-pixel phone widths. Sampled conservatory and labyrinth redraws
were around 8–14 ms in that test environment; this is not a physical-phone
frame-rate guarantee. WebGL rendering and physical Android performance
still need a device check. The test browser has no installed narrator
voices. Voice selection, late arrival, volume, and muting were tested with
simulated voices; an audible female narrator must be checked on the phone.
Audio activation and control state were verified, not speaker sound quality.
