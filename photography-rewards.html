Share the Memories

$10 for each of you.

After a referred new client completes a paid session, both the referring client and new client receive $10 in credit toward a future photography session.

Birthday Snapshot Club

Your birthday. Your choice.

Returning clients choose a $10 birthday credit or one complimentary extra edited photograph. Lifetime VIP members choose $15 credit or two extra edited photographs. One birthday reward per year, during the birthday month.

Memories Through the Years

Celebrate every third year.

Families receive 15% off every third qualifying annual session, repeating for the sixth, ninth, and later qualifying milestones.

VIP Shutter Club

Five sessions.
Lifetime thanks.

Five completed paid photography sessions unlock permanent VIP status, with priority booking consideration, exclusive offers, and one extra edited photograph with eligible sessions.

Capture Points

Every memory adds up.

Earn 1 point per eligible dollar paid. Redeem 100 points for $5 in photography credit. Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.

Keep it simple

One reward per booking.

Choose one eligible loyalty reward for each booking. The 50% deposit is calculated on the final discounted session price. Promotional combinations will be defined before the program launches.
