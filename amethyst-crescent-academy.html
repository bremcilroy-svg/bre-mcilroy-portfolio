Welcome to the Academy

Dear future student,

We’ve left the lanterns burning for you. Follow Gizmo’s pawprints from the silver gate to the castle door. The Royal Great Hall lies beyond the Moonlit Courtyard.

When you’re ready, step into the Library of Forgotten Words for your entrance trial. Four chambers await. Complete the trial and find the four hidden seals to reach the Author’s Study, where your house uniform and student key card are waiting.

We look forward to seeing which house you’ll call home.

The Headmaster
