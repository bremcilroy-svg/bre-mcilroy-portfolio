THE AUTHOR’S JOURNAL — Bre Lynn McIlroy

For your existing Author’s Hidden Room installation.
1. Extract poetry-vault-author-journal-update.zip.
2. Upload all SIX extracted files beside index.html in the GitHub Pages
   source folder. Replace matching filenames. Keep every other file.
3. Commit, wait for Pages deployment, then refresh the Author’s Hidden Room.
4. Enter using your existing completed Keeper save and choose Open the journal.

Includes all three supplied lyric documents, across twelve journal pages.
The first two PDFs had no title, so Untitled song I and Untitled song II are
working labels. Echos follows the supplied filename. Change titles in
vault-author-journal-data.js whenever ready. No lyrics were rewritten;
PDF page breaks were removed and text was repaginated for the reader.
This is a lyric-reading journal. No audio recordings were supplied or added.

CONTROLS
Choose a song in the contents, turn pages, enlarge the text, bookmark pages,
and jump back through Bookmarked pages. Your last page, bookmarks and text
size are stored in this browser under bre-poetry-vault-author-journal-v1.
Escape closes the journal and returns focus to its opening button. Arrow
keys turn pages when focus is in the reading area. Existing gameplay saves,
poem locks, author portrait and letter, and book shelf are preserved.
The journal is reached through the already gated Author’s Hidden Room.
As with the rest of the static game, this is a gameplay gate, not encryption.
