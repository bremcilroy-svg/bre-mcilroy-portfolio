# Default cat sound recordings

- `gizmo-meow.mp3` is “Meow.ogg,” Dan Crosby’s recording of Silas, from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow.ogg). Converted to mono MP3. The conversion is available under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `gizmo-purr.mp3` is adapted from [“Purring cat.oga” by Mysid](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Purring_cat.oga), released into the public domain. Trimmed, faded, and converted to mono MP3.
