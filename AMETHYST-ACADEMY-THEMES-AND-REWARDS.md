# Amethyst Crescent Academy — Theme and Treasure Preservation Plan

Status: **Design roadmap**, not a claim that all realms are playable. Preserve the original Poetry Vault and its existing locks, bookmarks, and saves.

## Academy identity
Royal medieval fantasy; purple, crimson red, silver, icy blue and amethyst; snow owls with blue eyes; Gizmo the gray-and-white castle cat; Headmaster Breezy. Atmospheric, explorable spaces with accessibility and mobile controls.

## Requested thematic inspirations
These are **future original fantasy experiences inspired by** the user's favorite genres and media, not reproductions of copyrighted characters, scripts, or assets.
- Vampire Diaries: gothic vampire court, moonlit ballroom, Victorian mysteries, supernatural keepsakes.
- Harry Potter: wizard-school discovery, spellcraft puzzles, enchanted corridors, magical studies.
- Princess / royal fairytales: royal wardrobes, tiaras, castles, enchanted gardens.
- Alice in Wonderland: surreal tea rooms, size and perspective riddles, whimsical doors.
- Once Upon a Time: storybook magic, dark-to-red enchanted hearts, fairytale quests.
- Wolves: Moonfang Forest, wolf tracks and moon-phase puzzles.
- Childhood toys: Kingdom of Forgotten Toys, playful mechanical puzzles.
- Basketball: Court of Champions, teamwork and route puzzles.
- Planets: Celestial Observatory, constellations and astronomy challenges.
- Other user favorites (Game of Thrones, Halloweentown, Disney princesses, Final Fantasy) remain possible inspirations for original future realm design.

## Current implemented treasure phase
- Four original Poetry Vault chapters: each has an easy exploratory chest and a secret chest.
- Secret chests require original chapter completion, its original hidden bookmark, and an independent 2/3/4/5-stage riddle sequence.
- Collectible inventory, cosmetic avatar-preview overlays, five badges, milestone rewards.
- Original saves remain authoritative. Treasure progress uses separate localStorage keys.
- Keepers' Hall and Amethyst Gateway milestone collectibles exist; Legendary Crescent Crown reserved pending a verified finale trigger.

## Next implementation priorities
1. Integrate chest placement with actual world-space positions and proximity, not fixed HUD icons.
2. Build distinct multi-step environmental secret mechanisms, with reusable hints and persistent partial stage progress.
3. Add optional themed realm chests, badges, and wardrobe sets without blocking the original ending.
4. Improve full-avatar wearable graphics and Gizmo accessories, with original artwork and animations.
5. Test all interactions in browser and on mobile before merging the development branch into main.

No purchases, loot-box randomness, or reward penalties for using hints.
