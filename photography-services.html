Booking details

A clear plan, before the camera.

Flexible appointments

Request a time on a Wednesday, Saturday, or Sunday, with at least 48 hours’ notice and up to 90 days ahead. Every appointment is reviewed and approved by Bre.

Deposit & confirmation

After approval, a signed agreement and a 50% deposit are required to confirm the session. The remaining balance is due at the appointment.

Local travel included

Travel within Genesee County is included. Travel outside the county is quoted separately before booking.

Weather flexibility

If weather makes an outdoor session unsafe or impractical, reschedule at no extra charge and transfer the deposit to the new date.

Rescheduling

Give at least 48 hours’ notice to transfer the deposit to a new available session date. Weather-related rescheduling is exempt from this notice requirement.

Payment preferences

Credit and debit cards, cash, and Cash App are the accepted payment methods to arrange with Bre. Tips at final payment are optional; no tip is preselected. This website does not collect payment details.
