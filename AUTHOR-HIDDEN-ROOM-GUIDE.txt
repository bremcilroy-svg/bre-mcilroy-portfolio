WHAT’S NEW
A candlelit illustrated author’s study with four interactive discoveries:
a welcome letter, planned-books journal, music box, and camera. On phones,
the same objects have large buttons below the scene. All four discoveries
earn The Author’s Mark, a downloadable SVG bookmark. It is an extra keepsake,
separate from the original twelve achievements and sixteen poems.
