1. Keep a backup of your existing GitHub repository.
2. Unzip this launch-update file on your device. Open the extracted folder.
3. In GitHub, open bremcilroy-svg/bre-mcilroy-portfolio at its root (the same folder as index.html).
4. Upload ALL files individually from this extracted folder. Replace files with matching names and keep unrelated photos, videos and portfolio files unchanged.
5. Commit the changes; allow GitHub Pages to deploy.
6. Open:

   https://bremcilroy-svg.github.io/bre-mcilroy-portfolio/poetry-vault-feedback.html

7. On Android, open the video with sound, test the phone movement controls, and check narration through the GAME's Settings. Game narration uses voices available on your device; trailer narration is not recorded in this version.

FEEDBACK AND GUESTBOOK — PRIVACY
The feedback page PREPARES an email to Bremcilroy@gmail.com. The player reviews it, then must press Send in their mail application. No messages are silently sent or stored by this website. If a device has no mail application, players can copy the draft and email it manually. Bug reports and messages without permission to publish must remain private.

PUBLIC GUESTBOOK APPROVAL
Guestbook is intentionally empty at launch. For a consenting player's review, open vault-guestbook.json and add a small owner-approved, spoiler-free excerpt. Example ONLY (not an actual review):
{
