Amethyst Crescent Academy

The Four Houses

Four traditions share one castle. Each house treasures a different kind of magic. Explore their crests and stories as the Academy grows.

🦉

House Amethyst

“Wisdom lights the hidden path.”

Wisdom · Imagination · Curiosity

Under violet banners and silver stars, House Amethyst keeps a quiet observatory where every question is welcome.

🐉

House Emberheart

“Courage begins with kindness.”

Bravery · Loyalty · Heart

Ruby tapestries and a warm hearth welcome the brave. Emberheart protects others and believes compassion is a kind of strength.

🐺

House Frostveil

“Steady through the longest night.”

Resilience · Patience · Unity

Beneath icy-blue windows, Frostveil gathers in a hall of winter constellations. Its members stand together through every storm.

🦌

House Silverthorn

“Grace grows where roots run deep.”

Creativity · Integrity · Wonder

Silver vines frame the entrance to a moonlit garden. Silverthorn values artistry, honesty, and the magic of creating something new.
